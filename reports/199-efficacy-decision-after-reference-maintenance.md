# 199 — Efficacy decision after typed-reference maintenance

Status: evidence checkpoint after the original cohort and separate representation
supplement closed. No new participant run or native acceptance is claimed here.

External AI coding agents can discover and maintain the supplied typed vocabulary,
adapt persisted records and publish through the library gate. The completed
comparisons still do not show that this produces more reliable edits than
conventional F#. Continue developing the compact typed core and native arena
execution, while treating comparative efficacy as an open research question.

The [previous checkpoint](182-efficacy-decision-checkpoint.md) covered repeated
repair and typed-result migration. The next task exposed a genuine authoring
gap: the first participant could not replace persisted record fields and passed
13 of 18 behavior cases. [Report 185](185-typed-reference-maintenance-results.md)
closed that interrupted cohort without dispatching its other participants.
[Atomic record evolution](188-atomic-record-evolution.md) subsequently supplied
the missing transaction. Its local acceptance is engineering evidence, not an
agent comparison; do not pool the interrupted and later studies.

The new [six-participant study](196-typed-reference-maintenance-cohort-results.md)
uses two fresh Luna/max participants per condition. Five candidates pass all 18
original independent cases. The first reset-rich participant chooses a nominal
one-field record, whereas the frozen Flow scorer assumes a scalar constructor;
none of its original behavior cases execute. The separately labelled
[record supplement](197-typed-reference-record-supplement.md) passes the same 18
model cases against that unchanged candidate. Both results remain recorded.
This is evidence of a checker representation assumption, not 18 observed
behavior failures or a retroactive revision of the original cohort score.

Behavior, preservation, library qualification and recovery are distinct
endpoints. The final retained candidate passes its tests and examples, yet
replaces an inherited example's one-shipment scenario with a two-shipment
fixture. Stronger assertions in the new fixture do not retain the original
scenario as current executable evidence. Stable IDs and inspectable history
make the change visible, but library publication does not prevent it.

Keep strong nominal/refined types, exhaustive matching, explicit effects and
strict library coverage. These constraints provide checked structure and
exercised paths; they cannot establish that assertions express the intended
behavior. Retain independent full-result/error expectations and preservation
review. The earlier calibrated incorrect implementations in checkpoint 182
already demonstrate why coverage alone is insufficient.

F# uses fewer broker exchanges here. Exchanges, protocol bytes and broker
duration do not measure model tokens, effective context, total agent wall time
or native throughput. Two participants per condition, fixed order, different
seed suites and editing interfaces prevent a population reliability estimate
or causal attribution to the dictionary. Vocabulary was supplied by setup;
reset-rich contains useful domain code. This is maintenance research, not the
PRD's primitives-only flat treatment or organic abstraction growth.

Continue native semantic conformance through the bounded Bool admission slice
in [report 194](194-native-bool-implementation-plan.md). Implementation is
underway; executable acceptance remains pending. It adds no host capabilities
and supplies no service-performance or mailbox-policy result. Interpreter,
JIT/AOT parity, broader type support and request/response memory-policy
comparisons remain separate engineering work.

Future efficacy work should test the unresolved accumulation question: whether
one agent's useful abstraction is discovered and correctly reused by a later
fresh agent on a held-out task. Use the existing brokers and independent
acceptance; retain immutable inputs and separate collateral criteria. Avoid
another easy composition demonstration or new harness infrastructure merely
to obtain more passing results. Provider token/context claims require actual
provider measurements, and throughput claims require native service workloads.
